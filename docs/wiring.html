Module notes

  • SX1278 LoRa (Ra-02): 3.3 V ONLY - 5 V destroys it. Fit the antenna before powering.
  • DHT22 temp/humidity: Bare sensor: 10k pull-up DATA->3V3 (modules have one).
  • HC-SR04 water level: 5 V module: ECHO must go through a divider.
  • IR flame sensor: Power it from 3V3 so DO is a 3.3 V signal.
  • MQ135 gas: Heater ~150 mA from 5 V, always on (no deep sleep).
  • Rain gauge (reed switch): Switch between the pin and GND.
  • Capacitive soil moisture v1.2: At 3.3 V supply AO stays below ~3 V.
  • MPU6050 tilt/vibration: I2C address 0x68 (AD0 to GND).
  • PMS5003 particulate: Fan needs 5 V; its UART is 3.3 V logic.
  • pH board (PH-4502C): Calibrate with pH 7 and pH 4 buffers ('p' command).
  • Turbidity (SEN0189): Output 0-4.5 V.
  • 18650 battery sense: Divider from battery + to the pin (high values: tiny drain).
  • Status LED: On-board LED on most DevKits.
