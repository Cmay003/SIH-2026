LANDSLIDE RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity: Continue monitoring ground tilt and vibration. No action
required.

MEDIUM severity: Alert the local disaster management authority and
public works department. Inspect the slope for new cracks, tilting trees
or poles, and sudden muddy water in streams. Advise residents to avoid
travel on hill roads near the affected slope.

HIGH severity: Evacuate houses on and directly below the affected slope
immediately and move people to stable ground away from the slope. Close
roads passing through the slide zone and coordinate with NDRF/SDRF teams.
Do not return until the area is declared safe by authorities.
