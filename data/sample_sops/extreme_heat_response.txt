EXTREME HEAT RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity: Continue monitoring temperature. No public action required.

MEDIUM severity: Issue a heat advisory. Residents should drink water
frequently even if not thirsty, use ORS, avoid strenuous outdoor work
between 12 noon and 3 pm, and wear light, loose cotton clothing. Check on
elderly people, young children and outdoor workers.

HIGH severity: Activate the local heat action plan. Open cooling shelters
and drinking water points, and alert health facilities to prepare for
heatstroke cases. Anyone with confusion, hot dry skin or fainting may have
heatstroke: move them to shade, cool them with water and call 112 or take
them to the nearest hospital immediately.
