FOREST FIRE RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity: Continue monitoring temperature and smoke sensors. No action
required.

MEDIUM severity: Alert local forest department and fire watch teams.
Restrict access to the affected forest zone. Prepare firebreak equipment.

HIGH severity: Dispatch fire response teams immediately. Evacuate any
settlements within 2 km of the detected hotspot. Coordinate with State
Forest Department and local fire services. Avoid the affected area until
declared safe.
