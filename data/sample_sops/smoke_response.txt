SMOKE RESPONSE SOP (sample - generic guidance written for this project,
not taken from a district SOP; needs review by a fire-safety / disaster
management expert before field use)

A smoke alert means fine particles (PM2.5) and combustion gas are rising
together at the sensor, which usually means something is burning nearby.
It does not say where the fire is or how large it is.

LOW severity: Continue monitoring PM2.5, gas, temperature and humidity.
No action required.

MEDIUM severity: Alert the local fire service and ask a nearby officer to
check the area around the sensor for a fire. Advise people nearby to stay
indoors with doors and windows closed. Children, elderly people and people
with asthma, lung or heart disease should avoid going outside.

HIGH severity: Dispatch the fire service to the area around the sensor.
Advise anyone who sees fire, or whose building is filling with smoke, to
leave by the safest route away from the smoke and call 112. While moving
through smoke, stay low, where the air is clearer. Keep everyone else
indoors with doors and windows closed until the area is declared safe.
