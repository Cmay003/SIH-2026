FLASH FLOOD RESPONSE SOP (sample - generic guidance written for this
project, not taken from a district SOP; needs review by a disaster
management expert before field use)

A flash flood alert means the river at the sensor is rising fast, even if
the water level is still low. There may be only minutes before the water
reaches the bank.

LOW severity: Continue routine monitoring of the river level and its rate
of rise. No public alert required.

MEDIUM severity: Notify the local disaster management authority. Advise
people near the riverbank, stream beds and low crossings to keep away from
the water and be ready to move to higher ground quickly. Watch for further
updates.

HIGH severity: Issue an immediate advisory for riverside and low-lying
zones. People near the river should move away from the bank and stream
beds to higher ground now, without waiting for the water to arrive. Do not
walk, swim or drive through flowing water, and do not cross causeways or
bridges with water over them. Authorities should coordinate with SDRF/NDRF
teams and keep people away from the river until it is declared safe.
Emergency number: 112.
