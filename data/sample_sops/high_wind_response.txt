HIGH WIND RESPONSE SOP (sample - generic guidance written for this
project, not taken from a district SOP; needs review by a disaster
management expert before field use)

A high wind alert is based on the weather forecast for the node's area;
SANJEEVNI nodes do not measure wind. It means strong gusts, or
gale-force wind as defined by the India Meteorological Department, are
expected within the next 24 hours.

LOW severity: Continue monitoring the forecast. No public alert required.

MEDIUM severity: Notify the local disaster management authority. Advise
people to secure loose objects such as tin sheets, signboards and roof
items, and to avoid standing under trees, hoardings, electric poles and
weak walls. Check official IMD warnings.

HIGH severity: Issue an advisory. People should stay indoors in a strong
building, away from windows, and keep away from trees, poles, hoardings
and fallen power lines. Do not travel unless necessary. Authorities
should prepare to clear fallen trees, restore power safely and keep
relief teams ready. Emergency number: 112.
