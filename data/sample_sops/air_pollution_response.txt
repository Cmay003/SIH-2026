AIR POLLUTION RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity: Continue monitoring PM2.5 and PM10. No action required.

MEDIUM severity: Issue an air quality advisory. Children, elderly people
and people with asthma, lung or heart disease should reduce prolonged
outdoor exertion. Authorities should check nearby sources such as open
burning and construction dust.

HIGH severity: Advise everyone to avoid outdoor exertion, keep windows
closed and wear N95 masks outdoors. Alert health facilities to expect
more respiratory complaints. Authorities should act against local
pollution sources such as open burning and uncovered construction dust.
