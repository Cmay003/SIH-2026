HEAVY RAIN RESPONSE SOP (sample - generic guidance written for this
project, not taken from a district SOP; needs review by a disaster
management expert before field use)

A heavy rain alert means 24-hour rainfall at or near the node is in, or is
forecast to reach, the India Meteorological Department's heavy rain range
(64.5 mm or more in 24 hours). A forecast-based alert has not been
measured by SANJEEVNI sensors.

LOW severity: Continue routine monitoring of rainfall, river level and
slopes. No public alert required.

MEDIUM severity: Notify the local disaster management authority. Advise
people to avoid unnecessary travel, keep away from rivers, streams and
steep slopes, keep drains clear, and keep a torch, phone charger,
drinking water and medicines ready. Check official IMD warnings.

HIGH severity: Issue an advisory for low-lying, riverside and hill-slope
areas. People should stay indoors in a safe building, avoid crossing
flooded roads, streams or causeways, and move away from slopes showing
cracks, falling stones or muddy water. Authorities should watch for flash
floods and landslides, keep SDRF/NDRF teams informed and be ready to open
relief shelters. Emergency number: 112.
