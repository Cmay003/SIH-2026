GAS LEAK RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity: Log reading, continue monitoring.

MEDIUM severity: Alert facility safety officer. Ventilate the affected
area. Restrict ignition sources nearby.

HIGH severity: Evacuate the affected industrial zone and surrounding
150-meter radius immediately. Shut off gas supply at the nearest valve if
safe to do so. Contact local fire and emergency services. Do not use
electrical switches or open flames in the area.
