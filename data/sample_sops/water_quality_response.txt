WATER QUALITY RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity: Continue monitoring pH and turbidity. No action required.

MEDIUM severity: Notify the water supply and public health engineering
department to test the source. Advise residents using this source to
boil drinking water (rolling boil for at least one minute) or use
treated water until results are available.

HIGH severity: Stop using the affected source for drinking and cooking.
Arrange an alternative safe water supply such as tankers. Collect water
samples for laboratory testing and alert health facilities to watch for
diarrhoea and other waterborne illness.
