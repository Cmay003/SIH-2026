FLOOD RESPONSE SOP

LOW severity (risk score below 0.4): Continue routine monitoring. No public
alert required. Log reading for trend analysis.

MEDIUM severity (0.4 to 0.7): Notify local disaster management authority.
Advise residents in low-lying areas near the riverbank to move valuables to
higher ground and monitor further updates. Keep drainage channels clear.

HIGH severity (above 0.7): Issue immediate evacuation advisory for
low-lying and riverside zones. Authorities should open designated relief
shelters and coordinate with NDRF/SDRF teams. Residents should move to
higher ground immediately and avoid crossing flooded roads or bridges.
Emergency contact: District Disaster Management Cell.
